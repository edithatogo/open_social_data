## Bounded review loop

A source/provenance review checks byte identity, rights and source dependence; an
HEOR review checks estimands, endpoints, perspectives, uncertainty and causal
identification; an engineering review checks tests, paths and compatibility.
These are documented reviewer roles, not claims that autonomous agents or named
external skills have run. Every material finding becomes a test or explicit
remaining gate. Avoid a second orchestration engine and repeated approval prompts.
